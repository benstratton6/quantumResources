In 2026 I took part in the Mongol Rally, with our two teams driving Fiat Pandas from Devon to the Far East Region of Kazakhstan.  

Here I give some advice I would have considered useful in hindsight, and some of my best photos from the rally. 

## Advice 

1. Buy your car a while before leaving.
    - Driving the car for sometime before setting off might allow small problems to come to the surface before you leave. 
2. Consider getting a Toyota. 
    - There will be more availability of parts in central asia, no guarantee you will find them of course, but it will be more likely than finding Fiat parts, for example. That being said, Fiat Panda's are epic cars so consider them too, especially the 4x4 version. 
3. Convoy with other teams where you can. 
    - Meeting and convoying with other teams led to some of the highlights of the rally. Moreover, if you breakdown its very helpful to have someone else around to tow you, get a tow truck or provide moral support. 
4. Try and learn something about cars before leaving. 
    - At least learn enough to know if an issue means you should stop immediately, or if its okay to push on. Its a big ask, but it would sure have been useful.
